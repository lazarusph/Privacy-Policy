# Privacy Policy for Moba Arena

Effective date: November 1, 2026


App: Moba Arena

Android package: com.moba.arena

## 1. About Moba Arena

Moba Arena is an Android app with mini-games, player profiles, leaderboards, coins, rewards, and redemption features. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, including guest and registered accounts.

This policy applies to Moba Arena, Android package com.moba.arena. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of Moba Arena identified in its Google Play listing. Our privacy contact is listed below.

## 2. Information we collect

- Account information: username, account identifier, account status and creation details, and an email address when you register or link an email account. Guest accounts also have an identifier.
- Sign-in information: your email address and password are processed through Firebase Authentication. Passwords are not stored as plaintext in the app’s profile or redemption database.
- Profile information: selected avatar, profile banner, description, game ID, server ID, and customization choices.
- Gameplay and rewards: game participation, results, scores, relevant play durations, multiplayer room and turn information, XP, rank, balances, reward claims, missions, check-in and spin records, and timestamps.
- Redemption information: account identifier, email if available, game ID, server ID, selected package, coin cost, request status, timestamps, and administrator messages or cancellation notes.
- Creator events: submitted video links, associated account details, participation records, submission status, and award details when you join an event.
- Operational and support information: app version, session identifier, recent activity and reward-event records, and information you provide when contacting us.

Service providers also process technical information as described below. Avoid including sensitive personal information in your username, public description, or submitted video links.

## 3. How we use information

- Provide sign-in, guest accounts, account recovery, and account management.
- Save progress and operate mini-games and multiplayer sessions.
- Display profiles, leaderboards, and event results.
- Manage coins, balances, missions, rewards, and redemption requests.
- Review suspicious activity, moderate content, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse.
- Respond to support and privacy requests, deliver advertising, and maintain the service.

## 4. Public profiles and administrator access

Other users can see your username, selected avatar and banner, profile description, coin balance, XP, and rank through leaderboards and player-profile views. Multiplayer participants can see the player identity and match details needed to play. Creator-event results can display a winner’s username, placement, prize amount, and award date.

Your email address, password, private redemption details, and administrator-only activity records are not intended to appear in public profiles. Authorized administrators can access relevant private records to manage accounts, review activity, moderate content, and handle rewards or redemptions.

## 5. Services we use and information sharing

- Firebase Authentication handles account sign-in and authentication. It processes authentication details and technical request metadata such as IP addresses and user-agent information.
- Firebase Realtime Database stores and synchronizes profile, gameplay, reward, redemption, event, and administration records.
- Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and Google Play services support advertising and related service functions.
- GitHub delivers public images, update information, and app configuration. Requests to GitHub disclose technical request information, such as an IP address, under its privacy practices.

We share information with these services for the functions described in this policy. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to investigate misuse or protect users. External websites and video services you open have their own privacy practices.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase privacy and security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Google advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## 6. Advertising

Moba Arena uses AdMob banner, app open, and optional rewarded ads. Google’s advertising SDK processes IP addresses, ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and App Set ID. An IP address can indicate a general location. The app does not request precise GPS location for these features.

Advertising-related processing supports ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, subject to the restrictions for each identifier. Moba Arena does not use App Set ID for ad personalization or ad measurement, or link it to personal information or other Android identifiers for advertising purposes.

Watching a rewarded ad is optional. Android’s advertising settings provide available controls for your advertising ID and advertising privacy. Changing these settings does not necessarily remove all ads.

## 7. Information stored on your device

The app and its SDKs store sign-in state, the last entered email address, game best scores, cached images and configuration, reward-retry information, and interface preferences on your device. Android backup and restoration may include some local data according to device settings.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app affects local data. It does not by itself delete your Firebase account or information already stored in the app’s online database.

## 8. Security and international processing

We use account authentication and database access controls to help protect private records. Firebase account and database connections use encrypted transport, and Google’s advertising SDK transmits user data using TLS. No internet service can guarantee complete security.

Google and GitHub may process information outside your country through their international infrastructure. Their privacy and data-processing terms describe how they protect information.

## 9. Data retention

Account and app records are kept for as long as needed to provide the account, save progress, administer rewards and redemption history, resolve disputes, protect the service, and meet applicable legal requirements. Retention depends on the purpose of the record and any applicable legal obligation.

Cached information can remain on your device until replaced or cleared. Service-provider logs and backups are subject to the provider’s retention practices.

## 10. Privacy requests and account data

For questions about your information, or requests for access, correction, or deletion where applicable, email lazarusgamingph@gmail.com with the subject “Moba Arena — Privacy Request”. Include your username and registered email address, or explain that you use a guest account. Provide your account identifier if known. Never send passwords or authentication codes.

We may ask for limited information to verify account ownership and handle requests in accordance with applicable law. Any necessary retention for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or a dispute is explained when responding to the request.

The current app does not include a self-service account-deletion screen. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete cloud account records. Privacy requests can be sent to the contact email above.
